CAMPUSOS ACADEMIC REGULATIONS 2026

Section 4.2: Attendance Requirement
Students must maintain at least 75% attendance in every course
to be eligible to appear for semester-end examinations.

Section 4.3: Attendance Shortage
Students below 75% attendance may be restricted from examinations
unless an approved exception is granted under institutional policy.

Section 6.1: Academic Event Leave
Students attending approved academic events may apply for event leave.
The request must be submitted before the event and remains subject to
faculty approval. Event leave does not automatically waive attendance rules.

Section 7.1: Maintenance Service Level Agreement
High-priority classroom equipment issues must be assigned within 4 hours.
Medium-priority issues should be addressed within 24 hours.

Section 8.1: Event Registration
Students may register for college events when seats are available.
Event registration is confirmed only after successful submission through
the official campus event system.
